abstract: 'To realize a crowd sensing campaign, sensing tasks with spatio-temporal requirements are distributed to the devices that can provide the requested information. Typically, this distribution relies on a centralized communication infrastructure such as cloud servers, which is unsuitable when access to such infrastructure is restricted, for example in disaster relief scenarios. This paper proposes a decentralized approach to task distribution using Named Data Networking (NDN) with context-aware geocasting to efficiently reach devices matching the spatio-temporal requirements of a sensing task.'
